8.2 The Employee shall become eligible for gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 upon completion of five (5) years of continuous service.

9. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS

9.1 Annual Leave (Privilege Leave)

The Employee accrues 1.25 days of annual leave per completed calendar month, totalling fifteen (15) days per year.

Leave must be applied for in advance and is subject to manager approval.

Annual leave may be accumulated up to a maximum of seventy-five (75) days. Any balance in excess of 75 days will lapse at year end.

Accrued but untaken annual leave (up to a maximum of 75 days) as at the date of termination will be encashed and paid out in the Full and Final Settlement.

9.2 Sick Leave (Medical Leave)

The Employee accrues 1.0 day of sick leave per completed calendar month, totalling twelve (12) days per year.

Sick leave is non-cumulative and any unused balance lapses at year end.

For any absence exceeding two (2) consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner must be provided.

Sick leave is not encashable upon termination.

9.3 Public Holidays

The Company observes a set of public holidays applicable to our India office. The approved holiday list will be shared with the Employee on their first day of joining and will be reviewed and updated at the start of each calendar year. The Company reserves the right to revise the holiday list from time to time based on operational requirements, with reasonable notice provided to all staff.

9.4 Maternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to maternity leave in accordance with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (as amended), subject to eligibility conditions.

9.5 Other Leave

Any other leave (including bereavement, marriage, or emergency leave) is at the Company's discretion. Such leave must be applied for in writing, and approval is not guaranteed.

10. COMPANY EQUIPMENT & ASSETS

The Company may issue equipment and assets to the Employee for use in performing their duties. All such assets are listed in the accompanying Fixed Assets Declaration, which forms a part of this Agreement.

The Employee agrees to use Company assets only for legitimate business purposes, maintain them in good working condition, and return them in accordance with the terms of the Fixed Assets Declaration upon termination or demand.

The cost of unreturned, damaged, or lost assets may be deducted from the Employee's Full and Final Settlement. The Company reserves all legal rights to recover the value of such assets.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION

11.1 During and after employment, the Employee must not disclose, use, or misuse any confidential information belonging to the Company or its clients. Confidential information includes:

- Client data, tax information, financial records, and personal information.

- Business strategies, pricing, processes, methodologies, and systems.

- Login credentials, software, and IT infrastructure details.

- Any information that is clearly of a confidential or sensitive nature.

11.2 The Employee shall handle all personal data in compliance with applicable data protection obligations under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

11.3 The Employee must immediately report any suspected data breach to the Company's management.

11.4 This obligation survives the termination of this Agreement.

12. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product, documents, software, tools, methodologies, inventions, and other materials created by the Employee in the course of employment are the sole property of the Company. The Employee assigns all such intellectual property rights to the Company. This is consistent with Section 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957 (India).

13. NON-SOLICITATION

During employment and for twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee agrees not to:

- Directly or indirectly solicit or approach any client or prospective client of the Company with whom the Employee had material dealings.

- Induce or attempt to entice away any employee of the Company.

14. POLICIES & CODE OF CONDUCT

The Employee agrees to comply with all Company policies, procedures, and codes of conduct as issued from time to time, including:

- Information security, IT acceptable use, and data handling policies.

- Anti-bribery, anti-corruption, and conflict of interest obligations.

- Workplace behaviour, anti-harassment, and equal opportunity standards.

Copies of applicable policies will be provided on commencement and updated periodically.

15. EXCLUSIVE EMPLOYMENT & CONFLICT OF INTEREST

15.1 The Employee agrees that their employment with the Company is on a full-time, exclusive basis. During the term of this Agreement, the Employee must not, without the prior written consent of the Company:

- Take up any other employment, contract work, freelance assignment, or paid engagement with any other employer, client, or third party.

- Operate, manage, or hold a financial interest in any business that competes with or conflicts with the interests of the Company.

- Use Company time, systems, equipment, or confidential information in the performance of any outside work or personal venture.

15.2 If the Employee wishes to undertake any voluntary, honorary, or unpaid activity outside of their employment (including advisory roles, non-executive directorships, or community work), they must disclose this to the Company in writing prior to commencing such activity. The Company will consider such requests reasonably and respond in writing.

15.3 Breach of Exclusive Employment Obligation:

If the Company discovers that the Employee has taken up outside employment or a conflicting engagement without prior written consent, the Company may, at its sole discretion, terminate this Agreement with immediate effect and without notice or payment in lieu.

Alternatively, where the Company determines the breach to be minor or unintentional, it may choose to initiate a discussion with the Employee and provide a reasonable opportunity to remedy the situation before deciding on any further action.

In either case, the Company reserves the right to recover any losses or damages arising from the breach of this clause.

16. TERMINATION

16.1 Notice Period

Following confirmation of employment (after probation), either party may terminate this Agreement by providing {{ doc.notice_period }} written notice. The Company may elect to pay salary in lieu of notice, calculated on a pro-rata basis for the unexpired notice period.

16.2 Termination for Cause

The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice in the event of:

- Gross misconduct, dishonesty, fraud, or wilful neglect of duties.

- Serious or repeated breach of confidentiality or data security obligations.

- Conviction of a criminal offence relevant to the Employee's role or reputation.

- Any act or omission constituting a fundamental breach of this Agreement.

- Breach of the exclusive employment obligations set out in Clause 15 of this Agreement.

16.3 Full and Final Settlement (FnF)

Upon termination for any reason, the Company shall process the Employee's FnF settlement within thirty (30) days, including:

- Outstanding salary up to the last working day.

- Encashment of accrued but untaken annual leave.

- Any other statutory entitlements due under Indian law.

The Company may deduct from the FnF any amounts owed by the Employee to the Company, including the value of unreturned Company assets as per the Fixed Assets Declaration.

17. LEAVE — ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS

17.1 All leave must be applied for and approved in advance by the Employee's reporting manager. Any leave taken without prior approval will be treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP) and will result in a salary deduction of one day's pay per day of unapproved absence. The Employee shall not be eligible to avail any leave during the probationary period, except for sick leave, which shall accrue from the date of commencement of employment. All other leave accrual shall commence from the date of confirmation of employment.

18. NOTICE PERIOD — ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS

18.1 The Employee does not have the right to buy out or unilaterally shorten the notice period. The Company may permit an early release at its discretion, but the effective notice period will in no case be less than forty-five (45) calendar days. Regardless of which party initiates termination, the Employee must satisfactorily complete all pending duties, client work, and project responsibilities, and provide a proper handover before the last working day. The Employee shall not be entitled to any allowance, benefit, or payment beyond what is expressly stated in this Agreement unless separately agreed in writing.

18.2 Consequences of Immediate Resignation Without Notice: If the Employee resigns with immediate effect and refuses or fails to serve the contractually required notice period without the prior written consent of the Company, the following consequences shall apply:

- The Company shall be entitled to withhold and forfeit salary equivalent to the unserved portion of the notice period from the Employee's Full and Final Settlement, as compensation for the shortfall.

- Any accrued but untaken annual leave that would otherwise be encashed at the time of separation shall not be paid out if the Employee fails to serve the required notice period.

- The Company reserves the right to recover from the Employee any additional costs, losses, or damages suffered as a direct result of the abrupt departure, including but not limited to costs of emergency replacement, client disruption, or project delays.

- The Company may initiate appropriate legal proceedings to recover such losses, and the Employee's breach of notice obligations may be reported to prospective employers upon reference checks, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

19. REMEDY FOR BREACH

In the event of a breach of any obligation under this Agreement, the Employee shall be liable to indemnify the Company for all costs, losses, and damages caused, including those arising from breach of confidentiality or misuse of intellectual property. The Employee acknowledges that such breaches may not be adequately compensated by monetary damages alone, and accordingly the Company shall be entitled to seek an immediate injunction or other equitable relief from a court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to any other remedies available under this Agreement or applicable law.
