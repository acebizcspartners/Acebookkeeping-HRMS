4. RETURN OF ASSETS

4.1 Resignation or Termination Within 12 Months of Joining

If the Employee's employment ends for any reason within twelve (12) months of the date of joining, all Company assets listed in this Declaration must be returned in good working condition. In this scenario:

- The cost of shipping, freight, or courier required to return the assets shall be borne entirely by the Employee.

- The Employee agrees to arrange and complete the return of all assets within seven (7) calendar days of the last working day.

4.2 Resignation or Termination After 12 Months of Joining

If the Employee's employment ends after completing twelve (12) months of continuous service, all Company assets must be returned in good working condition. In this scenario:

- The Company will bear the reasonable cost of shipping or courier required to return the assets.

- The Employee must return all assets within seven (7) calendar days of the last working day.

4.3 Return Condition

Assets must be returned in the same condition as issued, subject to fair wear and tear. Any damage beyond fair wear and tear will be assessed and the cost of repair or replacement may be deducted from the Employee's Full and Final Settlement.

4.4 Failure to Return Assets

If the Employee fails to return any Company asset within the required timeframe without reasonable justification:

- The Company reserves the right to deduct the estimated replacement value of any unreturned asset(s) from the Employee's Full and Final Settlement (FnF).

- Where the FnF settlement is insufficient to cover the full replacement cost, the Employee agrees that the outstanding amount constitutes a debt owed to the Company.

- The Company reserves the right to initiate appropriate legal proceedings under Indian law to recover the value of unreturned assets, including but not limited to civil proceedings for recovery of property and/or damages.
